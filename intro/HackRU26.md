# HackRU Fall 2026 · 比赛介绍 / Event Intro

HackRU 是 Rutgers University 举办的 24 小时黑客松，欢迎不同专业和经验水平的学生组队开发软件或硬件项目。

HackRU is a 24-hour hackathon at Rutgers University, welcoming students of all majors and experience levels to build software or hardware projects.

- **日期 / Dates:** October 10–11, 2026
- **地点 / Venue:** Busch Student Center, Rutgers University
- **时间 / Time zone:** 美国东部时间 / Eastern Daylight Time (EDT, UTC−4)

## 赛制 / Format & Rules

| 项目 / Item | 中文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 组队 / Teams | 每队最多 4 人；欢迎新手参加。 | Teams of up to 4 people; beginners are welcome. |
| 参赛资格 / Eligibility | 本科生、研究生均可参加；高中生须在活动时年满 18 岁。未满 18 岁不能参加。 | Open to undergraduate and graduate students. High school students must be 18 by the event; participants under 18 cannot attend. |
| 费用 / Cost | 免费参加。 | Free to attend. |
| 开发与评审 / Build & judging | 周六中午开始开发，周日中午提交截止，下午进行评审。 | Hacking starts Saturday at noon; submissions are due Sunday at noon, followed by afternoon judging. |
| 行为规范 / Conduct | 遵守 MLH Code of Conduct，尊重他人，禁止骚扰和辱骂。 | Follow the MLH Code of Conduct: be respectful; harassment and abuse are not tolerated. |

**赛道 / Tracks**

- **Social Good:** 改善社区的项目 / Projects that benefit the community.
- **Education:** 促进教育与学习的项目 / Projects that support education and learning.
- **Maverick:** 其他创意项目 / Any other creative hack.

官网另列有赞助商奖项；具体奖项要求以主办方公布的信息为准。

Sponsor prizes are also available; refer to organizer announcements for specific requirements.
