    BREATHLESS

    Your real lungs are the oxygen tank.

    1. Hold your breath in real life and your diver can dive.
    2. The deeper you go, the rarer the fish. Mythics only appear below 50 m, down in the trench.
    3. Breathe underwater and you are out of air: race to the surface or lose your net.
    4. Surface to bank your catch.

    Breath detection by Presage SmartSpectra · face and chest visible, good light

    Connecting to breath sensor…

    One shark by day. Two at night.

    They home in on your heartbeat. The faster it beats, the faster they charge and the more fish a bite tears from your net. Keep calm and they lose interest; they will not follow you to the surface.

    • Rarer fish. While sharks are out, every new fish rolls its rarity three times instead of the usual one by day or two at night and keeps the best.
    • No heart-rate reading. Dives with sharks in the water are left out of the heart-rate figures in your health report: a chase is not a resting measurement. Breath-hold times still count.

    Change it any time: Esc → Settings → Sharks.
